# 📄 CVigo — Free, Privacy-First Online CV & Cover Letter Builder

**Live App:** [https://cvigo.pages.dev/](https://cvigo.pages.dev/)

CVigo is a fast, zero-backend online resume (CV) and cover letter generator designed for job seekers across Europe and the DACH region. It runs entirely in the browser with **no sign-up, no watermarks, and no server-side data storage**.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Zero Registration & No Paywalls:** Build and export print-ready A4 PDFs immediately.
- **100% Local Privacy:** All personal information is saved exclusively in your browser's `localStorage`.
- **Multilingual Support:** Instant switching between **English**, **German (Lebenslauf / Anschreiben)**, **Bosnian**, **Croatian**, and **Serbian**.
- **12 ATS-Friendly Templates:** Clean layouts optimized for Applicant Tracking Systems and European HR standards.
- **Matching Cover Letter Generator:** Automatically generates a styled cover letter synced with your CV details.
